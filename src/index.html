Maker Tools

Browser tools for multi-color 3D printing. Everything runs in your browser; the 3MF files load as one object with filaments already assigned in Orca / Snapmaker Orca.

Choose a tool

Relief Maker

Address → terrain relief with contour lines or elevation-band / satellite colors, trails, POI markers, detailed water and a name plate.

Town Maker

Your street in 3D: buildings with roofs, roads, trees, fields and water. Your own house highlighted.

Fin Studio

Open an STL, OBJ, 3MF or PLY, find the best orientation and add clean breakaway support fins. Export as 3MF for OrcaSlicer or STL.
